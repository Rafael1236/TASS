Please rebuild the bot with two modes that the technician 
chooses at the start:

MODE 1 - QUICK (recommended):
The bot asks the technician to send a single voice note 
or text message describing everything about the visit. 
Then the system uses Claude AI to extract and map the 
information to these exact fields:
- tecnico_nombre (who did the service)
- cliente_nombre (client name)
- numero_llamada (SAP call number)
- numero_proyecto (project number)
- tipo_servicio (one or more of: Mantenimiento, Soporte, 
Instalación, Reparación, Cableado, Programación, 
Capacitación, Garantía, Estudio, Entrega)
- trabajo_realizado (description of work done)
- reporte_repuestos (list of materials used, each with 
quantity and description)
- reporte_tecnicos (auxiliary technicians, each with name 
and whether they are subcontract: true/false)
- hora_entrada (entry time HH:MM)
- hora_salida (exit time HH:MM)
- estado_servicio (one of: Terminado, Pendiente de 
repuesto, Requiere visita adicional, En proceso)
- hay_cotizacion (true/false - whether there is something 
to quote)
- descripcion_cotizacion (if hay_cotizacion is true)
- observaciones (additional notes for supervisor)

After extraction, the bot shows a summary card with all 
filled fields and asks only for the missing required ones 
one by one. Required fields are: tecnico_nombre, 
cliente_nombre, numero_llamada, tipo_servicio, 
trabajo_realizado, estado_servicio.

MODE 2 - GUIDED:
The bot asks each field one by one in Spanish using 
chips for fixed choices and free text for open answers. 
Same fields as above in this order: tecnico_nombre, 
cliente_nombre, numero_llamada, numero_proyecto, 
tipo_servicio, trabajo_realizado, reporte_repuestos, 
reporte_tecnicos, es_subcontrato, hora_entrada, 
hora_salida, estado_servicio, hay_cotizacion, 
descripcion_cotizacion (only if hay_cotizacion), 
observaciones.

BOTH MODES:
- WhatsApp dark UI look and feel
- Microphone button that records audio
- Typing indicator between bot messages
- Final summary card showing all fields before confirm
- Confirm and Edit buttons at the end
- All bot messages in Spanish
- The technician can send multiple voice notes or text 
messages and the system accumulates the information
